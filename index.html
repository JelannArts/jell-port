Terms of Service

Plain-language terms covering how projects, revisions, and payments work.

1. Overview

These terms cover custom logo design commissions through Jelann Arts. Starting a project means you agree to the terms below.

2. Services

Jelann Arts provides custom logo design: Standard and Professional packages, as described on the pricing page. No other design services are included unless separately agreed.

3. Pricing

Standard is $90 and Professional is $150. Rush orders add 25% of the base package price. Additional revisions add 10% of the base package price, per revision. All add-ons are calculated from the original base price, not a running total.

4. Payment

Projects require 50% payment upfront and 50% upon completion. Work begins once the upfront payment is received. Payments are non-refundable once work has started, except where stated otherwise in Section 12.

5. Revisions

This is the most important section, please read it. Revisions exist to refine the logo that was already created for your project: adjusting colors, refining typography, minor shape changes, adjusting proportions, small composition changes, or refining details.

A revision is not a way to request an entirely different project for the same price. Requests for a completely different concept, a new symbol, a new direction, or unrelated artwork are treated as a new project, which may require a new quote, even if it's for the "same" logo.

A revision round may contain multiple related adjustments submitted together as one consolidated round of feedback. Requests submitted separately or after a revision round has been completed may count toward the available revision limit.

6. Project Scope

Revisions are based on the original project brief and the direction you approved. If the brief changes after work has begun, or you request something substantially different from what was approved, Jelann Arts may treat it as a new project requiring a separate quote. Small, reasonable adjustments to the existing concept are not treated this way; this is a judgment call based on whether the request refines the existing work or replaces it.

7. Rush Orders

A rush fee (+25% of base price) secures priority scheduling and a faster turnaround. It does not include extra revisions or additional work beyond the selected package.

8. Final Files

Final logos are delivered as PNG by default. SVG is provided when technically appropriate and when the logo's complexity allows for a clean vector export. Editable source/working files (such as Affinity Designer files) are not included by default. Source files may be provided upon request and may be subject to an additional fee, depending on the project and file requirements. The designer is not required to provide internal working files, drafts, unused concepts, or other production files unless explicitly agreed upon before delivery.

9. Client Responsibilities

To keep a project on track, please provide a usable brief, relevant references, clear direction, and timely feedback. If direction changes substantially after a concept has been approved, the resulting work may be treated as a new project under Section 6.

10. Client Materials

The client is responsible for ensuring that any images, references, fonts, names, trademarks, characters, or other materials they provide for use in the project are authorized for use.

11. Project Inactivity

Projects may be paused when client feedback, required materials, or payment is delayed. Extended periods without client response may affect the estimated completion date and scheduling. Jelann Arts may reschedule or close an inactive project after reasonable notice.

12. Quote Relationship

The final project scope, package, price, add-ons, and agreed deadline are determined by the accepted quote and any written agreement between Jelann Arts and the client.

13. Cancellation / Refunds

If a project is cancelled before work begins, the upfront payment may be refunded. Any applicable payment-processing fees that cannot be recovered may be deducted from the refund. Once work has started, the upfront payment is non-refundable because it covers time and work already committed to the project. If Jelann Arts is unable to complete a project due to circumstances on the designer's side, the remaining unpaid balance will not be charged, and an appropriate refund of the upfront payment may be provided based on the amount of work completed.

14. Usage & Ownership

Upon full payment, the client receives ownership of the final approved logo design delivered to them and may use, reproduce, modify, and distribute it for their project, game, brand, and related commercial purposes. Ownership applies only to the final approved design and does not transfer until the commission has been paid in full. Jelann Arts retains ownership of unused concepts, rejected drafts, sketches, working/source files, and other unused design explorations unless otherwise agreed in writing. Jelann Arts may display the completed final logo in its portfolio, website, social media, and promotional materials unless a separate confidentiality agreement has been made.

15. Communication

Project communication happens primarily through Discord. Please keep feedback and responses reasonably timely so your project can stay on schedule.

16. Agreement

By submitting a quote request or starting a project, you confirm that you've read and agree to these terms, including the revision and scope policies above.
